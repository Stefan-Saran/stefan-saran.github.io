01 signal / why veil exists

Travel is full of names. Very few describe how a place actually feels.

Veil reads destinations like an atmospheric archive. Instead of asking for a city, a resort, or a checklist, it listens for a sensation: damp stone after rain, wind through firs, sodium light on empty streets, the hush before weather arrives.
